1. Overview (परिचय)

Bhoogol Atlas (भूगोल एटलस) is an educational geography application and website designed for students preparing for competitive examinations. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information shared while using our app or website (https://shankerb0003-debug.github.io/).

2. Information We Collect (एकत्र की जाने वाली जानकारी)
